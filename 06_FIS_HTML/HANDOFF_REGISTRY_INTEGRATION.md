# Handoff: plug the registry page into David-OS

From David, via Claude (coordinator), 2026-11-11. For Opus 5.5. Claude reviews when you are done.

## Where this is going

One registry page inside every folder of the Obsidian campus, generated by David-OS, in the style David
approved today. Alongside that, every JSON, Excel and HTML file moves into a dot folder that stays with its
folder, so Obsidian stops indexing it but it never leaves home.

You decide how it fits the FIS code. What follows is what exists, what David ruled, and what must not break.

## What exists (in this folder)

- `registry_scan.py`       reads a folder, writes one page. Standard library only. Read-only on the scanned folder.
- `registry_template.html` the page David approved ("winner winner"). Its flow copies the FIS Folder Brain page.
- `REGISTRY_SCAN.bat`      drag-a-folder launcher.
- `out\ZZZZ_Master_Folder_REGISTRY.html`  the approved example, built from the Math Is Moral folder.

It already calls the FIS engine (`file-intelligence-system\fis_folder_memory.py`) for the Master Equation
name, keywords, lossless address and proposed name. It does not write to the FIS database.

## What David ruled

- Clicking a file shows who it is (Master Equation name, UUID, ID, keywords, relations) and, under that,
  a row of boxes for how far it has come.
- A box is lit only when a receipt for that step was found on disk. Red means skipped (a later step is done,
  this one is not). Steps after the last finished one are not drawn.
- The record of a paper lives once. A paper shown in several folders (Quantum Paper) must not get several records.
- All JSON, Excel and HTML go into a dot folder beside the notes they belong to.

## Known gaps in what Claude built

- It only registers Markdown files that carry `id` and `uuid` in frontmatter, at the root or one folder down.
  A whole-campus version has to handle any depth and show notes with no identity yet (Identity box unlit).
- It writes one page for one root. "One page in every folder" is not built.
- The twelve steps and their order are Claude's proposal, not David's ruling:
  Identity, Draft, Audit, Deep evaluation, Knowledge graph, Lean, Review, Canon, Read aloud, Podcast, Video, Published.
- Review and Canon are read from the frontmatter `status` field. No paper has a review ruling recorded anywhere it can see.
- "Also shown in" is always empty: the Quantum Paper registry has no data yet.

## What must not break (Claude's objections, on the record for David)

1. STRUCTURAL: the C: drive had 0.00 GB free on Nov 11. The campus is on C:. Writing a page into every folder
   will fail part-way until space is cleared. Check free space before any write and stop cleanly if it is short.
2. STRUCTURAL: moving HTML breaks pages that link to each other or to sibling files. Published or built sets
   (Kimi's axiom series, anything under 50_PRODUCTION_AND_PUBLICATION, site builds) must be excluded or moved as
   a whole set. Frontmatter fields such as `card:` and `receipt:` hold relative paths and must be rewritten or
   still resolve after the move.
3. STRUCTURAL: never touch `.obsidian`, `.base` files or `.canvas` files. They are JSON and Obsidian needs them.
4. Moves follow the campus agreement: simulate first, then move with a receipt (source, destination, hash) and
   an undo. Nothing is deleted.
5. LOCAL: FIS already writes `FIS.html` into folders. Two generated pages per folder is one too many. Pick one
   name and one generator, and keep the generated page out of the move-to-dot-folder rule.
6. LOCAL: some folders hold tens of thousands of transcripts (the MIM.06 folder has 22,240). Cap listings; do not
   hash or classify every transcript on every scan.
