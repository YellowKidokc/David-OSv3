What we learned

Your system is project-heavy with a large mixed inbox pattern. The highest-value first move is not mass deletion. It is separating stable project folders from temporary intake folders, then applying naming profiles to the intake folders first.

  • Most files can be grouped by project, then by type.
  • Downloads and desktop-like folders carry the highest disorder score.
  • Several folders look like program roots and should be protected.
  • Duplicate clusters exist, but deletion should stay behind review gates.

What we recommend first

1. Protect program and system-looking folders. Mark as do-not-delete before any organization job runs.
2. Normalize names in the messy intake folders. Preview lowercase_underscore, Title Case, and date-project schemas.
3. Move only high-confidence batches. Let the user approve the prediction comparison before touching files.
