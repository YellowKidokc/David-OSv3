# WRONG TURNS / CORRECTIONS

This section collects all corrections, overclaims, and methodological boundaries identified across the eight source files.

## CORRECTION 1: The Two Starts Must Not Be Collapsed

**Source:** `07-two-starts-theology-and-science.md`

**Wrong turn:** Collapsing the theology start and the science start into one argument, as if science proves Trinity or Trinity is just a metaphor for scientific threes.

**Correction:** The two starts are genuinely different. Theology begins from revelation (top down). Science begins from the minimum conditions of inquiry (inside creation). They share a triadic grammar but operate in different registers. Neither should be reduced to the other.

## CORRECTION 2: Overclaiming What The Integral Proves

**Source:** `19-before-the-equation-scaffold-note.md`, `BEFORE_THE_EQUATION_creation_as_grammar_becoming_world_SOURCE_NOTE.md`

**Wrong turn:** Older language claimed the integral `chi = integral_Omega G(omega) K(omega) dmu(omega)` directly required physical time, matter-energy, observer, consciousness, and moral orientation.

**Correction:** The integral notation strictly requires only: a domain, a measure, typed functions, a product operation, measurability/integrability conditions, and an output space. Physical time, matter-energy, observer/record, and moral direction enter at different levels -- through dynamics, physical instantiation, measurement, and the theological orientation axiom respectively. The claim was too broad. The central intuition (structural generation) was good, but the formal scope must be honest.

## CORRECTION 3: Mathematical Non-Deception Does Not Directly Prove Moral Goodness

**Source:** `BEFORE_THE_EQUATION_creation_as_grammar_becoming_world_SOURCE_NOTE.md`

**Wrong turn:** An earlier version claimed that mathematical non-deception (correspondence, consistency) directly proves moral goodness, crossing the is-ought bridge.

**Correction:** The Principia ruling removed that bridge because correspondence and morality had been conflated. The cleaner route is: functional orientation -> conscious judgment -> moral responsibility -> theological identification of the ultimate good. "Math is moral" can remain as a later thesis, but the is-ought bridge has not been formally crossed.

## CORRECTION 4: The Analogy Between Postulates And I AM Statements Is Formal, Not Identical

**Source:** `12-primitive-postulate-proclamation-boundary.md`

**Wrong turn:** Treating the biblical `I AM` statements as if they were scientific postulates in the same ordinary sense, or treating scientific postulates as if they were personal divine speech.

**Correction:** The analogy concerns formal role only -- both are underived starting points tested by coherence, fruitfulness, explanatory reach, and resistance to collapse. The `I AM` statements remain historical-theological self-proclamation, not ordinary scientific postulation. The difference between a scientist proposing a postulate and a claim that reality's ground has spoken must not be erased.

## CORRECTION 5: Mathematics Is Not The Deepest Unifying Layer

**Source:** `BLUE-GRAMMAR-UNIFICATION-NOTE.md`

**Wrong turn:** Presenting mathematics as the root unifying layer that bridges theology and physics.

**Correction:** Grammar (the structure of valid derivation) is deeper than mathematics. Mathematics depends on prior conditions -- identity, distinction, relation, consistency, order, intelligibility, valid inference, non-contradiction, preservation across translation -- that it uses but does not generate. The correct unifying level is grammar, not mathematics.

## CORRECTION 6: Structural Generation, Not Mapping

**Source:** `BLUE-GRAMMAR-UNIFICATION-NOTE.md`, `BEFORE_THE_EQUATION_creation_as_grammar_becoming_world_SOURCE_NOTE.md`

**Wrong turn:** Treating the framework as arbitrary mapping (assigning theological terms to physics terms).

**Correction:** The framework performs axiomatic generation -- beginning with a declared ground and orientation, passing through constrained grammar, generating a structure whose available roles are demanded by the grammar itself. The slots are not chosen at random; they are structurally constrained. This is neither free mapping nor formal derivation; it is a third category.

## CORRECTION 7: Do Not Say "Science Proves Trinity"

**Source:** `07-two-starts-theology-and-science.md`

**Wrong turn:** Any of the following overclaims:
- science proves Trinity
- every three-part structure is Trinitarian
- triads are always more fundamental than dyads or larger structures
- information theory proves Logos
- measurement proves consciousness as soul
- physics proves God

**Correction:** Say instead: science discovers triadic grammar in the science register; theology names Trinity as the personal root; Theophysics studies the convergence without collapsing the registers. The correct sentence is: "science repeatedly discovers that stable, public, measurable, transmissible, or correctable reality often requires a triadic grammar; Christianity claims that ultimate reality is eternally triadic, personal, and self-grounding."

## CORRECTION 8: Creation Begins With Grammar, Not Matter

**Source:** `BEFORE_THE_EQUATION_creation_as_grammar_becoming_world_SOURCE_NOTE.md`

**Wrong turn:** Beginning the creation account with "matter suddenly appears."

**Correction:** Creation begins with "the grammar becomes physically instantiated." A physical world requires an admissible state domain, distinguishable states, lawful operations and relations, constraints, dynamics, actualization, stable records, and temporal order. Only then do space, matter, motion, and time arrive as mutually implicated faces of one created grant.
