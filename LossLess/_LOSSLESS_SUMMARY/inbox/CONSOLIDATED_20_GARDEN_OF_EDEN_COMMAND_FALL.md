# SOURCE 1: T-03 -- The Shadow Before Creation

**Status:** working canon draft
**Purpose:** explain how evil can be possible without making evil a created substance
**Register:** theology first, lived reality second, physics as witness, bridge declared

## The Question

If God is good, why is there evil?

That is the question everyone asks.

But it is not the first question.

The first question is sharper:

[CLAIM T3-1] How can evil be possible before anything evil exists?

If God is light, and in Him is no darkness at all, then evil cannot be a piece of God. It cannot be a hidden ingredient inside the Trinity. It cannot be one half of an eternal balance. Christianity has no room for that.

But if God is truly good, then goodness has direction. Love moves toward. Truth reveals. Life gives. Light illumines. The Good is not blank. It is ordered, alive, and oriented.

[CLAIM T3-2] And the moment towardness is real, away-from becomes intelligible.

Not actual.

Not created as a thing.

Not morally inhabited.

But intelligible.

That is the shadow before creation.

## The Clean Line

[CLAIM T3-3] Evil is not a created substance. Evil is the away-direction made possible by the reality of the Good, and made enterable only when finite freedom exists.

Before creation, the shadow is only a logical possibility.

After creation, the shadow becomes a moral possibility.

After refusal, the shadow becomes history.

## Split T.3.0 -- Shadow Is Not Evil

**Do not confuse:** the possibility of refusal with the act of refusing.

**Theology says:** God is light, and in Him is no darkness at all. God does not contain evil. God does not need evil. God does not create evil as a substance.

[SCRIPTURE: 1 John 1:5 -- "God is light, and in Him is no darkness at all."]

**Lived reality says:** a rule can be good before anyone breaks it. A warning can be loving before anyone ignores it. A boundary can create meaningful trust before betrayal exists.

**Physics-side witness:** an oriented system defines directions. A gradient defines uphill and downhill. A boundary defines inside and outside. A state-space can contain possible trajectories that are never actualized.

**Bridge status:** analogy / model language. Physics does not prove sin. Physics helps us distinguish possibility-space from actual event.

**Objection prevented:** "Are you saying God made evil?"

Answer:

> No. God made good real. The reality of good makes refusal intelligible. Free creatures made refusal actual.

## The Story

Imagine a father before the child has done anything wrong.

He puts his hand on the stove and says:

> Do not touch this.

[CLAIM T3-4] The command did not create burn. The command did not create rebellion. The command did not make the child evil. The command created a world where love could become trust.

Before the command, the child could not obey. Not really. There was no named good to trust. No boundary to receive. No chance to say, "I believe you."

But once the father speaks, two directions appear.

One direction is not dramatic. It is quiet trust. The child receives the word and lives.

The other direction is not a thing the father made. It is the away-from opened by the presence of a true toward.

The father did not need rebellion in order to love.

But love, once addressed to a free child, can be refused.

That is the cost.

Not evil as a substance.

Freedom as a real opening.

## Before The World

Before creation, there is no sin. There is no creature. There is no wound. There is no death. There is no betrayal.

There is only God: Father, Son, and Spirit. Life, light, love, truth. Perfect relation without loss. Fullness without hunger. Giving without depletion.

So where is evil?

Nowhere.

But where is the possibility of evil?

It is not in God as darkness.

It is not beside God as a rival power.

It is not beneath God as some second eternal principle.

[CLAIM T3-5] It is in the fact that the Good has direction.

If truth is real, falsehood is intelligible as deviation from truth.

If light is real, darkness is intelligible as absence of light.

If life is real, death is intelligible as loss of life.

If love is real, lovelessness is intelligible as refusal of love.

[CLAIM T3-6] The false depends on truth in a way truth never depends on the false. The shadow depends on light in a way light never depends on shadow. Evil depends on good in a way good never depends on evil.

That is the asymmetry.

That is why good and evil are not equals.

## Why Creation Changes The Question

[CLAIM T3-7] God can be perfectly good without evil. But a creature cannot be freely good unless it can receive the Good rather than merely instantiate it like a machine.

Creation introduces finitude:

- a creature is not God
- a creature receives life rather than originating it
- a creature has limits
- a creature can be addressed
- a creature can trust
- a creature can refuse

[CLAIM T3-8] Finitude is not evil. Dependence is not evil. Limitation is not evil. Need is not evil.

The child needing the father is not the fall. The branch needing the vine is not corruption. The receiver needing the source is not shame.

[CLAIM T3-9] The fall begins when dependence is reclassified as bondage.

That is the serpent's move:

> The gift is a trap. The boundary is control. The Father is withholding life.

[SCRIPTURE: Genesis 3:1-5 -- the serpent recodes the Father's command and character.]

Nothing external has to change first.

The relation is recoded.

Trust becomes suspicion.

Gift becomes scarcity.

Boundary becomes accusation.

Then the away-direction is no longer merely possible.

It is chosen.

## The Physics-Side Witness

Physics cannot say "sin."

Physics cannot say "God."

Physics cannot say "the serpent."

But physics can help us keep the structure clean.

### 1. Orientation Creates Possible Direction

A gradient does not force motion. It gives direction.

Once a hill exists, uphill and downhill are intelligible. Downhill is not a substance hidden inside the hill. It is a possible direction relative to the gradient.

[BRIDGE T3-B1] Goodness is not neutral being. It is oriented being. Once orientation is real, misorientation becomes possible.

### 2. State-Space Is Not Event

Physics distinguishes possible states from actual states.

A system can have many possible trajectories that never occur. Possibility belongs to the structure. Event belongs to history.

[BRIDGE T3-B2] The shadow can be possible before it is actual. Evil can be intelligible before it is chosen.

### 3. Boundary Makes Trust Possible

A boundary condition does not merely restrict a system. It gives the system a meaningful domain.

Without boundaries, there is no inside, outside, path, aim, or violation.

[BRIDGE T3-B3] The command in Eden is not arbitrary restriction. It is the boundary that lets trust become visible.

### 4. Closed Systems Run Down

In thermodynamics, a closed system cannot maintain ordered life indefinitely without exchange. Living things persist by remaining open: receiving, metabolizing, exchanging, repairing.

[BRIDGE T3-B4] The fall is not God injecting punishment into a neutral machine. It is the creature attempting to become closed against the source of life.

### 5. Noise Is Not Signal's Equal

Noise can corrupt a message only because there is a message to corrupt. Error is parasitic on information. Falsehood is parasitic on intelligibility.

[BRIDGE T3-B5] Evil does not create a world. It edits, distorts, consumes, and reclassifies the good world it receives.

## Theological Claim

The Christian claim is not:

> God made evil so the story would be interesting.

The Christian claim is:

[CLAIM T3-10] God made love possible, and real love required free reception. Free reception made refusal possible. Refusal made evil actual. Grace was not plan B; the Lamb was slain from the foundation of the world.

[SCRIPTURE: Revelation 13:8 -- "the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world."]

That means the Cross is not divine damage control.

[CLAIM T3-11] The Cross is the eternal character of God entering the actualized shadow to destroy it from the inside without destroying the freedom that made love possible.

God did not discover the cost after creation.

He created knowing the cost.

And He did not assign that cost finally to the creature.

He entered it.

## Why This Reaches People

Everyone knows this story before they know doctrine.

Every child knows the feeling:

> I knew what was right, and I did the other thing.

Every parent knows the ache:

> I gave the rule because I loved you, not because I hated your freedom.

Every person knows the strange fact:

> The wrong thing often begins by making the good thing look like oppression.

That is why this page may need to come before the machinery.

The reader does not need to understand entropy yet.

They only need to understand the stove.

The rule did not create rebellion.

The rule created trust-space.

The child created betrayal.

And the father still moved toward the child.

## Objection T.3.1 -- Did God Create The Away-Direction?

In one sense, no.

God did not create evil as a thing.

In another sense, God created a world where orientation is real, and real orientation makes deviation intelligible.

That is not a defect in goodness.

It is the cost of goodness being more than force.

[CLAIM T3-12] If God had created only beings incapable of refusal, there would be no moral evil. But there would also be no creaturely love in the sense Scripture cares about: trust, obedience, faithfulness, repentance, covenant, and chosen communion.

The away-direction is the price of addressable freedom.

The Cross is the price God pays to redeem it.

## Objection T.3.2 -- Does This Make Evil Necessary?

No.

[CLAIM T3-13] Possible does not mean necessary. Enterable does not mean entered. Foreknown does not mean authored as evil.

The framework must hold those distinctions or it collapses.

Evil is not required for God to be good.

Evil is not required for love to exist within God.

Evil is not required for truth to be true.

But the possibility of refusal follows from finite freedom addressed by real Good.

That possibility becomes actual only when a creature turns from trust into self-enclosure.

## Objection T.3.3 -- Why Not Create Free Beings Who Always Choose Good?

That question may be the hardest one.

The first answer is humble:

> We do not know every possible world God could have made.

But the framework can say this much:

[CLAIM T3-14] If a choice is guaranteed from outside the chooser in such a way that refusal is impossible, then the creature may enact goodness, but it does not receive and return goodness as free trust.

The world God makes is not merely a display of correct behavior.

It is a communion story.

Communion requires real otherness.

Real otherness requires a receiver who is not simply the source.

A real receiver can close.

And if it closes, the source must either abandon it, override it, or redeem it.

Christianity says God chooses redemption.

## The Visual Substrate

If this becomes a picture, do not paint evil as a monster beside God.

Paint light so real that direction appears.

Paint a boundary that feels like care before it feels like restriction.

Paint a child, a flame, a warning, and the silence before the hand moves.

The shadow should not look powerful.

It should look derivative.

It should exist only because something good is present to cast it.

## Cross-Resolution Status

**Theology Register:** strong. This page protects divine goodness by denying evil independent substance and placing evil's actuality in creaturely refusal.

**Lived-Reality Register:** strong. The parent/child boundary story is immediately intelligible.

**Physics Register:** modest. Gradients, state-spaces, boundaries, openness, and noise support the structure by analogy. They do not prove the doctrine.

**Bridge Register:** declared. The bridge is not "physics proves the fall." The bridge is "physics gives clean language for possibility, orientation, boundary, openness, and parasitic corruption."

**Mathematical Register:** not yet formalized. Possible future Lean target: distinguish `Possible`, `Actual`, `Good`, `Deviation`, and prove that deviation depends on a prior standard while the standard does not depend on deviation.

## The Line To Carry Forward

> The shadow was possible because the Good had direction. The shadow became enterable because love made free receivers. The shadow became history because trust was refused. And grace was already moving before the first hand reached for the flame.

# SOURCE 4: 01-the-story.html -- "God in the Equations" (Sheet 1 of 6, POF 2828)

**Full title:** Sheet 1 -- God in the Equations
**Subtitle:** A journey from physics to faith through the language of reality. No PhD required -- just the willingness to look.
**Byline:** De Revolutionibus Veritatis, David Lowe, POF 2828

## The Question That Changes Everything

Have you ever looked up at the stars and felt something? Not just wonder -- a pull, like the universe is trying to tell you something?

For thousands of years we've had two ways of understanding reality. **Science** tells us how things work -- gravity pulls, light travels, energy flows. **Faith** tells us why things matter -- love endures, truth liberates, grace saves. But what if they're not two different stories? What if they're the same story, written in two languages?

This page isn't clever arguments for God. It's something that's been hiding in plain sight: the fingerprint of the Divine in the mathematics of reality itself.

## The Language Beneath Language

Imagine a letter arrives in the mail. The paper is real. The ink is real. But what makes it *mean* something isn't the paper or the ink -- it's the information carried in the patterns. That's what the universe is: patterns carrying meaning.

[CITATION: Claude Shannon, 1948 -- the mathematics of information itself.]

In 1948, Claude Shannon found the mathematics of information itself and showed that meaning has laws, just like matter does. And here's the breakthrough: information is the one thing that exists in both worlds at once. In physics it's bits, entropy, signal and noise. In faith it's truth, revelation, the Word.

[SCRIPTURE: John 1:1 -- "In the beginning was the Word."]

When John writes "In the beginning was the Word," that is a claim about the substrate of reality -- made two thousand years before anyone had the math to check it.

## The Dual-Language Table

| Physical reality | Spiritual reality | Information language |
|---|---|---|
| Energy & matter | Divine action & form | Structure -- information density |
| Entropy (disorder) | Sin (spiritual decay) | Loss of coherence |
| Gravity (curvature) | Sin's pull toward self | Meaning bent inward |
| Light | Truth / revelation | Perfect transmission, zero noise |
| Observation | Faith | Possibility collapsed into reality |

[CLAIM S-1] These aren't similar. They're the same thing described in two languages. Physics is the vocabulary. Information is the grammar. God is the Author.

## The Master Equation

[EQUATION E-1]

```
chi(X) = C_W[ product(X_i) ]
```

Coherence is the product of nine normalized factors -- gravitation, mass-energy, electromagnetism, strong force, thermodynamics, information, quantum, relativity, weak force -- wrapped by the coherence operator. One zero anywhere kills the product.

## Ten Mirrors

### 1. Gravity <-> Grace

[CLAIM S-2] Mass curves spacetime; the apple isn't pulled, it follows the curve. Sin works identically -- a curvature in your reality that makes falling feel natural, everything bending back toward *me*. And grace is the counter-curvature. When it touches you -- a kindness, a moment of clarity -- suddenly you can move again. Same mathematics, two domains.

> Gravity gives you ground to stand on. The fall isn't punishment -- it's positioning. And grace is always stronger than gravity.

### 2. The Strong Force <-> Divine Love

[CLAIM S-3] Inside every atom, protons that should repel each other hold together anyway, because a force a hundred times stronger than the repulsion binds them. Love is that force in the relational domain -- the "I choose you" that holds what differences and hurts should tear apart. And like the strong force, it only works at close range.

[SCRIPTURE: John 15:4 -- "Abide in me."]

> The nucleus holds because the force never lets go. Your soul holds because His love never lets go.

### 3. Light <-> Truth

[CLAIM S-4] Light travels at one speed for every observer, carries information in every photon, and reveals whatever it touches. Truth behaves the same way: constant, indifferent to opinion, unstoppable.

[CITATION: Shannon -- channel capacity limited by noise.]

Shannon proved that what limits any channel is noise -- and your capacity to receive God is your signal-to-noise ratio. When God feels distant, the signal didn't weaken. The noise went up.

> You can close your eyes, but the light is still there. Prayer isn't making God louder. It's making yourself quieter.

### 4. Entropy <-> Sin

[CLAIM S-5] The Second Law is the cruelest-sounding truth in physics: in any closed system, disorder always wins. Cars rust. Bodies age. Stars burn out. And left to yourself -- a closed system -- you will always drift toward disorder. Not because you're weak. Because that's the law.

[SCRIPTURE: Jeremiah 17:9 -- "The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked."]

Jeremiah wrote it twenty-five centuries before thermodynamics: the heart is deceitful and beyond cure.

But the law has a loophole: it only binds *closed* systems. Open the system -- let energy flow in from outside -- and order can grow. That's how life exists at all. Grace is the energy from outside the system. You can't generate it, earn it, or manufacture it. You can only receive it.

> Entropy says everything falls apart without outside help. The system is never truly closed -- unless you close it yourself.

### 5. The Observer <-> Faith

[CLAIM S-6] In the double-slit experiment, a particle behaves as a wave of possibilities until it's measured -- then one outcome becomes real. Observation participates in reality. Faith is that act in the moral domain. Standing at a crossroads, every future overlaps and paralyzes; the moment you commit -- choose, forgive, step -- the wave collapses and one future becomes actual.

[SCRIPTURE: Matthew 9:22, Mark 5:34, Luke 8:48 -- "Your faith has healed you."]

> Possibility isn't reality until someone chooses. That's quantum mechanics. That's faith. Same architecture.

### 6. Motion <-> Repentance

[CLAIM S-7] A body keeps its state until a force acts on it. A soul keeps its trajectory -- habits, appetites, direction -- until acted on from outside. Repentance is not self-generated redirection; it is response to applied force. Newton's First Law, applied to the soul: you don't change because you decided to. You change because something from outside your inertia touched you.

> Inertia is the physics of stubbornness. Grace is the physics of intervention.

### 7. Electromagnetism <-> Witness

[CLAIM S-8] Maxwell's equations say electric and magnetic fields generate each other -- a self-sustaining wave that propagates forever. Truth and witness work the same way: truth revealed generates testimony, testimony generates encounter with truth, and the cycle self-propagates across space and time. Remove either component and the wave collapses.

> Light is a relationship between two fields that keep each other going. So is evangelism.

### 8. Information <-> Logos

[EQUATION E-2]

```
C = B * log2(1 + S/N)
```

Shannon's channel capacity theorem. The amount of truth you can receive depends on your bandwidth, your signal strength, and your noise floor.

[CLAIM S-9] Scripture, prayer, creation -- different channels, same Author, same information. The Word is not metaphor. It is the substrate claim: information is fundamental, and the source of all information is a Person.

> The signal is always broadcasting. The question is how much noise you're generating.

### 9. Relativity <-> Grace-Frame
