# Canonical filename decision

## Decision

Stage 2B uses the proposal emitted by `engine/rename_planner.py`:

```text
DOMAIN__CT__tag1-tag2__YYYYMMDD__ST.ext
```

Examples:

```text
TH__PAP__AXM-PRF__20261103__RV.md
PH__DOC__me3-KNW__20261103__AC.md
CD__COD__SRV-DBS__20261103__AC.py
```

For Master Equation enrichment, `meN` is prepended to the tag segment. It is metadata context, not a canon or verification declaration.

## Why this scheme

- It is generated by the maintained propose-only planner.
- Domain, content type and lifecycle status occupy separate fields.
- Short controlled codes remain sortable and grep-friendly.
- The tag segment can carry ME context without changing the surrounding contract.
- Applying a proposal remains a separate, approval-bound operation.

## Deprecated schemes

| Previous form | Stage 2B mapping |
|---|---|
| `DOMAIN__TYPE__words_in_lowercase__YYYYMMDD__ST.ext` | Map `TYPE` to `CT`; map words to controlled tag codes |
| `SERIES-NN_slug_CODE-CODE_xOPP.ext` | Preserve series/work identity in the registry/sidecar; map semantic codes to tags; do not add lifecycle to opposition fields |
| June FIS codes-only draft | Map domain/content/status to current controlled fields; regenerate tags through the maintained planner |

Existing files are not automatically migrated merely because a scheme is deprecated. They receive proposals and remain unchanged until an explicitly confirmed rename batch.

## Do not

- Do not infer canon status from a filename.
- Do not infer admission lane from a proposed name.
- Do not rename `HOLD` or `EXCLUDE` artifacts.
- Do not overwrite collisions.
- Do not treat preference confidence as permission to mutate.
- Do not drop work/version/path lineage during a rename.
- Do not edit `engine/rename_planner.py` to implement filesystem mutation.
