## 6. STRUCTURAL SEMANTICS

chi expresses coherence as coupled preservation of ordered relation.

high chi:
external ordering input present,
alignment strong,
signal faithful,
entropy effectively bounded,
time integration durable,
compression lossless enough,
phase regime stable or properly transformed,
superposition resolved without control-lock,
nonlocal correlation intact,
integration high.

low chi:
closed system,
misalignment,
signal corruption,
entropy leakage,
temporal fragmentation,
lossy compression,
unmanaged phase transition,
collapsed possibility or frozen control,
relational decoupling,
disintegration.
