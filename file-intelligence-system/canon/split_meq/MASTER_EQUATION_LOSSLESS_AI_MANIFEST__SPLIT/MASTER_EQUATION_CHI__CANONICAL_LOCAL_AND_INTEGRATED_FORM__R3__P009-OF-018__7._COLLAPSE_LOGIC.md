## 7. COLLAPSE LOGIC

PRODUCT_COLLAPSE:
If any necessary factor approaches zero, chi approaches zero.

INTERPRETATION:
A system may retain local strengths while losing total coherence because product coupling makes missing channels catastrophic.

EXAMPLES:
truth without love -> signal may remain but integration fails
knowledge without alignment -> K high, M low
institution without trust -> structure exists, F/R/C weakened
doctrine without grace -> K high, G low, C degraded
energy without order -> activity high, S_eff low
