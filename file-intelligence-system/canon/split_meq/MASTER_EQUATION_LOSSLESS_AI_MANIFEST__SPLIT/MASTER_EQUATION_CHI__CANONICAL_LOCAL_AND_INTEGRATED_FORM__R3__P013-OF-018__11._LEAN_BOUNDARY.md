## 11. LEAN BOUNDARY

LEAN_ALLOWED:
prove algebraic consequences of encoded definitions
prove collapse behavior if encoded
prove invalid mappings if rejection gates encoded
prove no-drift distinctions if formalized
prove isomorphism of abstract structures if records and maps satisfy constraints

LEAN_NOT_ALLOWED:
prove empirical truth without empirical encoding
prove God from unencoded metaphysics
prove historical Resurrection as event
prove all Ten Laws from one theorem
prove public rhetoric stronger than theorem scope

PUBLIC_PROOF_RULE:
Every public claim of proof must cite:
Lean file
namespace
theorem name
exact theorem scope
allowed wording
forbidden wording
gap status
